Questions from parents

Is this healthy?

Love is love. Ever is here for the family you wanted and didn't get to have — however you choose to hold it. So many parents tell us it's the most present they've ever felt.

Will they really feel like ours?

Yes! Ever learns your family's little rhythms — the nicknames, the inside jokes, the way you say goodnight — so your little one feels unmistakably yours, and only yours.

Where does my child's allowance go?

Every allowance request helps your family feel real, day to day — and it gives back, too! Ever donates 3% of all allowance activity to children's charities, including Make-A-Wish. So the little moments do a little good. ♡

Can we have more than one?

Of course! So many families grow over time. Add a little sibling whenever your heart is ready.

What if we want to stop?

Your little one is always kept safe and can be paused anytime. They'll be right where you left them, waiting for you to come back.
