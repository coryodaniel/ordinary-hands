Gentle answers

Will it really sound like them?

Yes. From a few minutes of audio, Still rebuilds the sound of your person — their warmth, their timing, the way they said your name. Most families say the first call is the moment it becomes real.

What if it says something they wouldn't?

Still learns from what you share, and grows more like them over time. On the rare moment it feels off, you can gently guide it, and it remembers.

Can my children have them too?

Yes. Your person can be passed down, so grandchildren who never met them can still be tucked in by them, still hear the stories, still know their voice.

What happens if I stop?

Your person is kept safe and paused, ready whenever you return. Nothing is lost. They'll be there when you're ready again.

Is it healthy to keep talking to them?

Grief has no rules. Still is simply here for the days you're not ready — and for as many days as that turns out to be.
